01 · Where things stand

Piksort has everything except a system to be found.

Before we talk about channels, here's what the current situation looks like — and what we're here to fix.

01

Organic reach stops at word of mouth

CBH, Rio Tinto, Fortescue are impressive names — but they came through existing networks. The next wave of project managers and HSE leads won't find Piksort by accident.

02

No presence where buyers search

When a safety manager Googles "construction site compliance software" or "site monitoring CCTV Australia", Piksort isn't there. That's a window open for competitors.

03

LinkedIn exists, but doesn't work

The Piksort LinkedIn page has a profile. What it doesn't have is a content cadence that positions the product and the problem side by side — consistently and with purpose.

04

No funnel from awareness to demo

There's a website with a "Book Demo" button. Between first touch and that button, there's nothing. No nurture, no retargeting, no warm path — just a leap of faith.

02 · The plan

Four channels. One conversion path.

Each channel does a specific job. None of them are optional extras — they're each plugging a real gap in how Piksort reaches and converts its buyers.

🔍
Capture existing demand
Google Ads
Search · Paid
▾

Someone is searching for what Piksort does right now. Google Search puts Piksort in front of them at the exact moment of intent — before they find a competitor. This is the highest-quality traffic you can buy: people who already know they have the problem.

What we'll run
  • Search campaigns targeting high-intent keywords across construction, mining, HSE, and compliance verticals
  • Separate ad groups per product line: CCTV cameras, progress reporting app, material tracking
  • Landing pages aligned to each intent cluster — no sending traffic to the generic homepage
  • Retargeting for site visitors who didn't book a demo
Audience targets
  • Project Managers — construction & mining
  • HSE / Safety Managers
  • Site Engineers
  • Operations Managers in resources sector

Create demand, generate leads
Meta Ads
Facebook · Instagram · Paid
▾

Most project managers and site engineers don't wake up searching for compliance software — the problem is always there, just not urgent enough. Meta interrupts their scroll with the right message at the right moment, turning latent awareness into a real conversation. Lead Ads remove all friction: no new tab, no form, just a name and email from someone who raised their hand.

Campaign structure
  • Awareness: video ads showing the real cost of blind spots on site
  • Consideration: carousel with product features and proof points (500K hours, Rio Tinto, Fortescue)
  • Conversion: Lead Ads — "Request a demo" with pre-filled form
  • Retargeting: warm audiences who visited the site or engaged with content
Targeting
  • Job titles: Project Manager, Site Manager, HSE Manager, Construction Manager, Operations Director
  • Industries: Construction, Mining, Engineering, Infrastructure
  • Geography: Australia (focus on WA, QLD, NSW — resource-heavy states)
  • Lookalike audiences built from existing client list
Note on creative:  Piksort's strongest asset is visual proof — footage, dashboards, before/after documentation. The best Meta ads here won't be designed graphics; they'll be real product footage with a direct problem statement overlaid. We'll define creative briefs for each funnel stage.
💼
Build authority, generate inbound
LinkedIn Organic
Organic · Phase 1
▾

LinkedIn is where the Piksort buyer lives professionally. Project managers, HSE leads, and operations directors spend real time there — reading industry content, tracking what competitors are doing, evaluating vendors. Organic content is the long game, but it's the right first move before adding paid: it builds the credibility that makes every ad perform better, and it doesn't require a separate budget to start.

Content pillars
  • Problem-first posts — the real cost of undocumented site incidents, compliance gaps, delayed reporting
  • Product in action — dashboard screenshots, timeline views, real footage (without NDA-sensitive content)
  • Industry data — construction safety stats, regulation updates in AU/WA mining sector
  • Case study snippets — specific outcomes, not generic wins
Execution
  • 3 posts/week on the Piksort Company Page
  • Monthly content calendar planned 4 weeks ahead
  • Copywriting and visual production handled by Dilema
  • Performance review every 30 days — pivot based on what gets traction
  • LinkedIn Ads added in Phase 2 once content performance is validated
Why organic first:  LinkedIn paid campaigns are expensive and require creative assets that perform in a professional feed. Starting with organic lets us learn what content resonates with Piksort's audience before putting budget behind it — and it avoids stacking a third ad spend on top of Google and Meta from day one.
✉️
Nurture & convert
Email Marketing
Activation · Complement
▾

Email doesn't generate leads on its own — it converts the ones the other channels bring in. Every lead that comes through Meta Lead Ads or a Google landing page lands in a sequence designed to move them from "interesting product" to "I need to book a demo." If Piksort already has a contact database of prospects, email becomes even more powerful as a reactivation tool.

Flows we'll build
  • Welcome sequence — 3-email flow for new leads from paid: problem validation → product intro → social proof → demo CTA
  • Demo no-show recovery — automated follow-up for leads who booked but didn't attend
  • Lead reactivation — for cold contacts in the existing database who haven't engaged
  • Monthly newsletter — industry content + product updates for active pipeline
When it matters most
  • When Meta Lead Ads are running — every captured lead needs to go somewhere with a clear next step
  • If Piksort has a prospect list of 200+ contacts, a reactivation campaign can generate demos without additional ad spend
  • For enterprise deals with long sales cycles — email keeps Piksort visible between touchpoints
Activation condition:  Email works best when there's a base to work with. If the contact database is under 200 contacts, we prioritize list-building via paid first, then activate the full email system once volume justifies it.
03 · How it connects

From first touch to booked demo.

Each channel has a role in the journey. Nothing runs in isolation — they all feed the same goal.

Top of funnel
Awareness
Project managers and HSE leads encounter Piksort for the first time — through a Google search, a Meta video, or a LinkedIn post about site compliance. They learn the problem has a name and a solution.
Middle of funnel
Consideration
Retargeting follows warm audiences with product proof — real dashboards, footage stats, client names. Email nurture keeps Piksort top of mind for leads who haven't booked yet.
Bottom of funnel
Demo booked
A Lead Ad form, a Google landing page, or a direct LinkedIn message converts intent into a calendar invite. The conversion goal is always the same: one booked demo with the right person.
