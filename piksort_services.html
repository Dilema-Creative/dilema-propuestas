02 · What's included

Four channels.
Fully managed.

Each service is designed to do a specific job in the funnel. Nothing is filler — every deliverable is connected to the goal of generating qualified demo requests.

🔍
Capture existing demand
Google Ads
Search · Paid
▾

Google captures buyers who are already looking for what Piksort does. Search campaigns put the product in front of project managers, HSE managers, and site operations leads at the exact moment they're searching for a solution — before they find a competitor.

Setup — included from month 1
  • Google Ads account configuration — full structure, billing, and access setup
  • Google Analytics 4 — property creation and event configuration
  • Google Tag Manager — container setup and deployment on piksort.com
  • Keyword Planner — research across construction, mining, HSE, and compliance verticals
  • Conversion tracking — demo requests, form submissions, key site actions
  • Negative keyword list — filters out irrelevant traffic from day one
Ongoing management
  • Weekly optimization — bids, keywords, ad copy, match types
  • Campaign structure — separate ad groups per product line: CCTV cameras, progress reporting app, material tracking
  • Search + Retargeting — capture intent and recover warm visitors who didn't book
  • Biweekly report — real metrics with interpretation, not just numbers
  • Monthly meeting — results review, strategy alignment, next steps
  • Ongoing copy iterations — ad text updated based on CTR and conversion data
Ad spend is separate.  What Piksort pays Google to show ads goes directly to Google — it's not included in the management fee. We'll suggest a starting investment level based on your demo volume goals.
📱
Build brand + generate leads
Meta Ads
Facebook · Instagram · Paid
▾

Meta reaches construction and mining professionals before they're actively searching — while they're scrolling. It does two jobs at once: builds brand awareness among the right decision-makers, and generates qualified leads through Lead Ads with conditional screening questions that filter out weak contacts before they even hit the CRM.

Setup — included from month 1
  • Business Manager configuration — accounts, pixels, domains, access structure
  • Meta Pixel + CAPI — conversion tracking and server-side event matching
  • Full funnel campaign structure — Awareness / Consideration / Lead generation
  • Lead Ads with conditional questions — filters leads by role, project type, and team size before submission
  • Creative brief — we define what content is needed per stage so production is intentional
Ongoing management
  • Content pieces for organic and campaigns — designed for both LinkedIn and Meta, adapted per platform format and communication goal
  • Brand building creatives — awareness content to position Piksort among site operations professionals
  • Retargeting — re-engage warm audiences who visited the site or interacted with content
  • Weekly optimization — audiences, creatives, copy, budget allocation
  • Lookalike audiences — built from existing client contacts to find similar buyers
On creative:  Piksort's best asset is real footage — site cameras, dashboards, before/after documentation. The best-performing Meta ads here won't be polished graphics; they'll be real product evidence with a direct message. We define the brief, Piksort provides the raw material.
💼
Authority + inbound
LinkedIn Organic
Organic · Phase 1
▾

LinkedIn is where Piksort's buyers operate professionally. Project managers, HSE leads, and operations directors spend real time in their feed — tracking industry news, evaluating vendors, and following companies they trust. Organic content builds that trust consistently, and makes every paid channel work better by the time someone clicks an ad.

Deliverables per month
  • 8 content pieces — 2 posts per week, published on the Piksort Company Page
  • B2B-focused communication — written and formatted for a professional construction and mining audience
  • Monthly content calendar — planned 4 weeks ahead, aligned to product, industry events, and campaign messaging
  • Copywriting + visual production — fully handled by Dilema, no internal effort required
Content pillars
  • Problem-first posts — the real cost of undocumented site incidents and compliance gaps
  • Product in context — dashboard views, timeline records, real use cases
  • Industry data — construction safety stats, AU/WA mining compliance updates
  • Case study snippets — specific outcomes, not generic wins
  • 30-day performance review — pivot based on what generates engagement and reach
Why organic first:  LinkedIn paid ads are expensive and need proven creative assets before spending. Starting with organic lets us validate what content resonates with Piksort's audience — and avoids stacking a third ad spend on top of Google and Meta from day one. LinkedIn Ads join the plan in Phase 2.
✉️
Nurture + convert
Email Marketing
Activation · Complement
▾

Email doesn't generate leads on its own — it converts the ones paid channels bring in. Every lead captured through Meta Lead Ads or a Google landing page enters a sequence designed to move them from "interesting product" to a booked demo. If Piksort already has a list of prospects, email becomes a zero-additional-spend activation tool.

Setup
  • Perfit account creation — platform setup, domain verification, sender configuration
  • Prospect list export + import — existing contacts cleaned, segmented, and loaded into Perfit
  • Welcome sequence — 3-email automated flow for new leads from paid: problem → product → proof → demo CTA
  • Lead reactivation campaign — for cold contacts in the existing database
Ongoing
  • Weekly campaigns — once the list is live and the system is configured
  • Demo no-show recovery — automated follow-up for leads who booked but didn't attend
  • Monthly newsletter — industry content + product updates for active pipeline contacts
  • Performance tracking — open rate, CTR, demo conversion per campaign
Activation condition:  Email works best when there's a base to work with. If the contact database is under 200 contacts, we prioritize list-building through paid first — then activate the full email system once volume justifies it.
